**ABOUT PAGE**

**About OKEFF Integrated Limited**

OKEFF Integrated Limited is an international business advisory and
investment facilitation company headquartered in Mississauga, Ontario,
with operations in Lagos, Nigeria.

We support investors, businesses, project owners, financial
institutions, manufacturers, commodity traders and government-related
stakeholders seeking credible commercial opportunities and strategic
partnerships.

Our role is to help bridge the gap between opportunity and execution. We
identify suitable partners, support transaction development, facilitate
introductions and provide advisory services that help clients move
opportunities forward.

OKEFF operates across oil and gas, trade finance, international trade,
commodities, carbon markets, vessel leasing and business development.

**Our Mission**
