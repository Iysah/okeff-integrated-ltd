**2. COMPANY OVERVIEW**

OKEFF Integrated Limited is a Canadian-based advisory and investment
facilitation company specializing in connecting investors, project
developers, governments, and financial institutions with high-value
opportunities across Africa and global markets.

The company provides strategic advisory services across:

- Investment Facilitation

- Trade Finance

- Oil & Gas Advisory

- Commodities Trading

- Energy Projects

- Capital Raising

- Strategic Partnerships

- Government & Public Sector Advisory

Our strength lies in identifying opportunities, structuring
transactions, mitigating risks, and connecting the right partners to
achieve successful outcomes.

**Our Vision**
